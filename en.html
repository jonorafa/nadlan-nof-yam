About the Neighborhood

Just a 5-minute walk from the sea, these neighborhoods are the next big thing in North Tel Aviv. Everything here is built to a high modern standard, with plenty of new buildings and contemporary architecture suited for those seeking a high quality of life.

The neighborhood is lively and developing rapidly. New cafes, restaurants, and local businesses are constantly opening, giving you everything you need right downstairs.

A Smart Future Investment

But the biggest plus here is the transportation. The new Metro project (already under construction) will pass right through the neighborhood.

This means you can live in the quiet of the north of the city, close to the sea, and reach Rothschild Boulevard and the center of action in a 7-minute drive. This is a smart investment where property values will only rise.
